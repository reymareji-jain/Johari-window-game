THE HIDDEN SELF

A game-based Johari Window experience. Explore how you see yourself, how others may experience you, what you choose to share, and what you might discover through new challenges.

⏱ 25–40 minutes👥 Individual + peer feedback🎯 Self-awareness & teamwork

Welcome, Insight Explorer

Imagine you have joined a new organization. You will face workplace situations, complete short tasks, reflect on your habits, and optionally invite a trusted peer to give feedback. At the end, you will receive a personal reflection profile organized around the four Johari Window areas.

🟩 Open Self

What you know about yourself and others also know.

🟧 Blind Self

Patterns others may notice that you have not noticed yet.

🟦 Hidden Self

What you know about yourself but choose not to share.

🟪 Unknown Self

Strengths or reactions that may emerge through new experiences.

Important: This is an educational reflection activity, not a psychological test or diagnosis. The Blind Self cannot be reliably identified from self-report alone; peer feedback is optional and should be respectful, specific, and voluntary. You may skip any personal question.

Peer Mirror: What might others see?

If you feel comfortable, ask a trusted classmate who has worked with you to respond based on observed behaviour. You may complete this privately, leave it blank, or use a fictional character for classroom practice. Avoid labels such as “lazy” or “rude”; describe actions and their impact.

You do not need to reveal sensitive personal information. Feedback should focus on observable, changeable behaviours and be offered with consent.
